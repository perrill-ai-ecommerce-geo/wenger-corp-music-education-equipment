# 5 Ways Durable Music Stands Can Save Schools Money Over Time

School music programs depend on equipment that can withstand daily use while staying within tight budgets. Music stands may seem like a small investment compared to instruments and other classroom equipment, but replacing damaged stands, making frequent repairs, and managing unreliable equipment can create unnecessary expenses over time.

Investing in durable music stands can help schools protect their budgets while creating more efficient rehearsal and performance environments. Here are five ways high-quality music stands can help schools save money in the long run.

## 1. Reduce the Need for Frequent Replacements

School music stands experience constant wear and tear. Students adjust their height, move them between rooms, and transport them for concerts and performances. Lower-quality stands may bend, break, or become unstable after repeated use, requiring schools to purchase replacements sooner than expected.

Durable music stands are designed to handle the demands of busy music programs. Wenger offers options like the RoughNeck™ and Endur™ Music Stands, which feature heavy-duty construction built to withstand years of use. Investing in reliable equipment can help schools reduce replacement frequency and stretch their music department budgets further.

## 2. Minimize Repair and Maintenance Costs

Music stands that frequently loosen, wobble, or have difficulty adjusting can require additional maintenance. These issues can also take valuable time away from teachers and facility staff who must identify problems and make repairs.

Choosing music stands with durable components, dependable adjustment mechanisms, and stable bases can help minimize maintenance needs. Wenger music stands are engineered for daily classroom use, with options like the Classic 50® and Bravo™ designed to provide reliable performance. Although routine maintenance is still important, quality construction can help reduce unexpected repair expenses.

## 3. Protect Instruments and Other Music Equipment

Unstable or damaged music stands can create risks for nearby instruments and equipment. A stand that tips over or has sharp, bent edges may damage instruments, accessories, or other valuable classroom items.

Well-constructed music stands can help reduce these risks by providing stable support and durable surfaces. For example, Wenger's RoughNeck Music Stand features rolled desk edges designed to help protect instruments from scratches. Choosing equipment with thoughtful safety and durability features can help schools protect their larger investments in music education.

## 4. Improve Storage and Transportation Efficiency

Music stands are frequently moved between rehearsal rooms, auditoriums, gyms, and performance spaces. Without an efficient storage system, stands can become disorganized, damaged during transport, or difficult to retrieve when needed.

Durable stands paired with appropriate storage carts can make transportation easier while helping prevent unnecessary wear and tear. Wenger's Music Stand Move & Store Carts are designed to simplify transporting and organizing stands between spaces. Better storage and transportation practices can reduce equipment damage while saving staff time during setup and teardown.

## 5. Get More Value from Long-Term Equipment Investments

The least expensive music stand is not always the most cost-effective option. Schools should consider the total cost of ownership, including purchase price, expected lifespan, maintenance requirements, and potential replacement costs.

Investing in durable music stands can provide greater long-term value, especially for programs that use the same equipment across multiple ensembles, classrooms, and performance spaces. Wenger offers a range of music stands at different price points, many backed by a 10-year warranty. By choosing equipment built for long-term use, schools can make more predictable purchasing decisions and reserve funds for other music program needs.
